About A I D E N

No two sets the same. Read the room, then move it.

A I D E N plays progressive house as AIDEN MUSIC, from the shores of Negombo, Sri Lanka.

He has played B2B with @roscoe.ofc at Rabbit Hole and with NATIV at NC3 Events' ASTROLINE in Negombo, joined the Eclipse Weligama lineup at W15, and talked through his journey on Episode 2 of Adieu's Podcast Series.
